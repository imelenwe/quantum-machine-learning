1. The network

2 inputs, 2 hidden neurons, 1 output, fully connected. A number sits on every line: its weight. Each neuron also has a bias. These 9 numbers are the knobs training turns. The target is t = 1.

Each hidden circle holds two numbers: z (left) and h (right). The ? marks fill in as you get them right in exercise A.

2. The equations

These are your four equations. Every name here is on the diagram.

nameformulain words
z₁w₁₁·x₁ + w₂₁·x₂ + b₁neuron 1's weighted sum
h₁tanh(z₁)neuron 1's output; it travels along neuron 1's outgoing line
z₂, h₂w₁₂·x₁ + w₂₂·x₂ + b₂,  tanh(z₂)the same for neuron 2
ŷv₁·h₁ + v₂·h₂ + cthe prediction ("y-hat"), no tanh
L(ŷ − t)²the loss: how far the prediction is from t = 1

Weight names: w₁₂ = from x₁ into neuron 2 (first number = where the line starts, second = where it ends). v₁ = from neuron 1 into the output. tanh squashes any number into −1 … 1.

3. Exercise A: forward pass, by hand

Work out these six numbers in this order, with the knob values on the diagram. Use a calculator for tanh. Keep 3 decimals. Within 0.002 counts.

5. Exercise B: backward pass, by hand

A slope for every name, right to left. Each one uses a slope you have just worked out, times one number from exercise A. Correct answers appear on the diagram in teal. Within 0.005 counts.

6. One learning step

Each knob moves a little against its slope: new = old − 0.1 × slope (0.1 is the learning rate, the step size). Then the forward pass runs again and L should be smaller.

No hand calculation here: press Show the answers in exercise B (or finish it) and these tables fill in automatically.
